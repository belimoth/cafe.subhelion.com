# DREAD CHECK

DREAD CHECK is a anthology of smaller games that share elements.
- First-person shooter, immersive simulation, survival, sandbox
- Science fiction, cosmic horror, conspiracy, time travel
- Collaborative play, cooperative play, splitscreen, friendslop
- Gamepad-first and experimental control schemes

There are two entry points into gameplay within DREAD CHECK:
- Action Mode: 5-15 minute commitments where you are summoned to to a single mission in a random setting
- Adventure Mode: 45 minute or longer sessions in a single setting, with the ability to save and load for multiple sessions. Adventure Mode players can summon Action Mode players to help them complete missions within their adventure.

Technical goals:
- Low-fidelity low-resolution presentation to reduce the cost of development and lower the barrier to entry for new chapters and community content
- Lowest possible hardware requirements and broad compatibility
- Small download size
